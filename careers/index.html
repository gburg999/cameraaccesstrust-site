Founding Software Engineer

Full-time · US remote-first (US-based required)

How to apply
About CameraAccessTrust

CameraAccessTrust, Inc. is building privacy-preserving authorization and audit for protected camera and license-plate (ALPR) data. Patent pending. We’re hiring a Founding Software Engineer to own the core backend: access control, audit integrity, APIs and integrations. This is a hands-on role working directly with the founder, not a manager seat.

About the role

You’ll design and ship the systems that authorize and audit access to protected camera and ALPR resources in a privacy-preserving way. Expect ownership of architecture and production code, close work with the founder, and integration work with outside camera and ALPR ecosystems.

What you’ll do

  • Design and build the core authorization flows for protected camera and ALPR data
  • Build audit capabilities that support verifiable review of those actions under privacy constraints
  • Own secure backend services and APIs used by operators, integrators and applications
  • Integrate with camera and ALPR vendor APIs and identity sources as the product needs
  • Set practical security and reliability habits: code review, secrets handling, deployment hygiene
  • Threat-model access paths and keep the build honest to privacy-preserving authorization and audit
  • Work directly with the founder on technical tradeoffs, sequencing and scope
  • Document interfaces and runbooks so the system can be demoed, integrated and audited

What we’re looking for

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering, including substantial backend or systems work
  • Hands-on experience with authorization, access control, identity, security-critical backends, or high-integrity audit and logging systems
  • Strong API design skills and comfort integrating third-party systems
  • Production experience in at least one backend language (for example Go, Rust, Java, TypeScript/Node or Python). Systems judgment matters more than the language.
  • Comfort working as a senior individual contributor in an early-stage company with light process
  • Clear written communication
  • US-based, with US work authorization

Nice to have

  • OAuth2/OIDC, SAML or enterprise identity-provider integrations
  • Background in security, privacy, public safety, government or other regulated environments
  • Cryptographic attestation or verifiable-logging experience
  • Prior time as an early engineer at a seed or pre-seed startup

Compensation

Competitive early-stage cash plus meaningful equity.
